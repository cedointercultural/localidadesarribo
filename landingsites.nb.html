Goreference landing sites for fishery logs

Get catch from official record. Filter out catch for artisanal fisheries and eliminate aquaculture production. Uses table with corrected names to standarize names of locations and fishery offices and correct incorrect state names. Combines catch files for all years and selects landing site, state, fishery office, species, weight, and catch value. Resulting data is imported as catch rmd into the package
